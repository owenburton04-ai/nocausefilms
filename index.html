Storytelling

Hi friend, welcome.

My name is Mckay Meacham.

I've always been drawn to the power of storytelling through film.

After graduating film school, I began creating wedding films because I felt there was an opportunity for something more cinematic. Films that do more than document a day, but tell a story.

Inspired by the emotion, artistry, and timelessness of cinema, I approach every wedding with the goal of preserving not just what happened, but how it felt.

NO CAUSE was built on the belief that not everything meaningful needs an explanation. You don't need permission to create, to explore, to love, or to live honestly.

I've always been inspired by James Dean.

Not because he had all the answers, but because he lived with an honesty that couldn't be manufactured. That same authenticity is what I look for on a wedding day. The quiet glances, imperfect moments, and genuine emotions that make every story uniquely its own.

It's about preserving a love that doesn't need to be justified or performed. A love that simply exists. My goal is to create films that feel just as real. Timeless, honest, and deeply human.

Maybe there is NO CAUSE.

Maybe that's the point.
