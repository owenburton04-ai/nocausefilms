Common Questions

Which package is right for our wedding?

Every wedding is different. Promise is designed for the wedding day itself, Forever allows the story to extend beyond a single day, and No Cause Signature is built to capture the full wedding experience across multiple events. I'm always happy to help you decide what fits your celebration best.

Can we customize a package?

Absolutely. Each package is designed as a starting point and can be tailored to your wedding. Additional coverage, filmmakers, films, Super 8 and other options can be added to create the right experience for you.

How does travel work?

Utah travel is included with Promise, all domestic travel and accommodations are included with Forever, and all worldwide travel and accommodations are included with No Cause Signature. Any travel outside your package is quoted separately with no hidden costs.

What is required to book our date?

A signed agreement and 30% retainer plus travel fee (if applicable) officially reserve your date. The remaining balance is due one week before your wedding.
